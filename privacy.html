Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. About Global Quant

Global Quant is a personal quantitative investment research and financial data management application. It is not offered as a public service.

2. Information Accessed

With the Google account owner's authorization, Global Quant may access application-related files in Google Drive, including its SQLite database and backup files.

3. How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used solely to store, retrieve, update, and back up the application's financial research database.

4. Storage and Security

Application data is intended to be stored in the account owner's private Google Drive. OAuth credentials will be stored as GitHub Actions secrets and will not be published in the application's public repository.

5. Data Sharing

Global Quant is not designed to sell or share Google user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner controls the files stored in Google Drive and can delete them at any time. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact the application developer using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
