Global Quant

Global Quant is a personal quantitative investment research and financial data management project.

Purpose

The application collects publicly available financial market data and stores research datasets in a private Google Drive folder.

Google Drive Access

Google Drive access is used to store, retrieve, and update the application's SQLite database and related backups. Access requires authorization from the Google account owner.

Availability

This application is intended for personal use and is not offered as a public service.
